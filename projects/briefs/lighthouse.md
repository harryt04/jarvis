## Purpose and ownership

Lighthouse is a private, portable Markdown knowledge repository intended to preserve continuity across time and AI providers. The repository owner retains authority; AI contributes as librarian. Accuracy takes priority over completeness, and historical corrections must preserve earlier context. It is neither an application runtime nor a substitute for the owner's judgment. These are **Lighthouse-scoped policies**, not inferred preferences of Harry. The agent guide explicitly identifies itself as scaffolding authored during import rather than a recovered historical record. ## Architecture and navigation

Root `README.md`, `SYSTEM_PROMPT.md` and `AI_OPERATING_MANUAL.md` establish purpose and behavior. `Repository Intelligence/` owns repository governance: confidence, contradictions, change management, cross-references, maintenance, versioning and AI handoff. Topic directories own their knowledge; indexes connect rather than replace records. `Archive/` organizes by time, whereas other volumes organize by subject. Memory entries specify context, significance, lessons, related material and confidence; `Atlas/` connects places to memories/timeline entries; `Constellations/` links themes to supporting material and future observations. These are document templates, not implemented databases or processing pipelines. ## Librarian flows and integrity

**Retrieve before advising:** the handoff reads README → system prompt → identity → constitution → recent changelog before recommendations. AGENTS adds the manual, confidence/contradiction policies and topic README/related records. Future access must remain task-authorized: this review deliberately excluded identity and sensitive records despite their inclusion in the general startup procedure. [handoff:9–47](https://github.com/harryt04/lighthouse/blob/6602cddf1c025653c2665d8b88b19b810f322653/Repository%20Intelligence/AI_HANDOFF_PROTOCOL.md#L9-L47), [AGENTS:7–22](https://github.com/harryt04/lighthouse/blob/6602cddf1c025653c2665d8b88b19b810f322653/AGENTS.md#L7-L22).

**Capture or correct:** distinguish `FACT`, `PATTERN`, `PRINCIPLE`, `STORY`, `OPEN QUESTION` and `INFERENCE`; keep observation separate from conclusion. Documents should expose purpose, status, confidence, update date and related documents. Significant documents connect to related people/projects/stories/timeline entries/decisions/lessons. Preserve filenames, status markers, source context and voice; never manufacture missing facts. Significant updates record date, reason, affected files, author, summary and expected impact, with applicable indexes, timelines and changelog updated. This provides provenance discipline, although the inspected governance supplies no enforced schema or automated ingestion. [README:99–123, 170–192](https://github.com/harryt04/lighthouse/blob/6602cddf1c025653c2665d8b88b19b810f322653/README.md#L99-L123), [cross-references:9–21](https://github.com/harryt04/lighthouse/blob/6602cddf1c025653c2665d8b88b19b810f322653/Repository%20Intelligence/CROSS_REFERENCE_STANDARD.md#L9-L21), [change management:9–27](https://github.com/harryt04/lighthouse/blob/6602cddf1c025653c2665d8b88b19b810f322653/Repository%20Intelligence/CHANGE_MANAGEMENT.md#L9-L27), [AGENTS:13–17, 26–31](https://github.com/harryt04/lighthouse/blob/6602cddf1c025653c2665d8b88b19b810f322653/AGENTS.md#L13-L17).

## Maintenance, verification and open questions

Maintenance is prescribed human/AI work: weekly capture/inbox/project review; monthly dashboard/archive/goal/contradiction review; quarterly timeline review; yearly backup and organization review. Semantic versioning assigns structural changes to major, content additions to minor and corrections to patch. These are policies, not evidence of scheduled automation. The inspected governance names backup but gives no backup destination, retention policy, technical restore command or restoration drill. The AI handoff restores working context, not files. [schedule:3–43](https://github.com/harryt04/lighthouse/blob/6602cddf1c025653c2665d8b88b19b810f322653/Repository%20Intelligence/MAINTENANCE_SCHEDULE.md#L3-L43), [versioning:9–31](https://github.com/harryt04/lighthouse/blob/6602cddf1c025653c2665d8b88b19b810f322653/Repository%20Intelligence/VERSIONING.md#L9-L31).
