## Intended process flows, all plans

1. **Delegation and plan approval.** A human moves a card into “Ready for Ants,” or uses its label equivalent. Ants plans/decomposes it and waits for human approval; moving it back to that state approves the next step. The proposed expanded board adds “Ants working,” “Needs input,” “Plan review,” “Human review,” and “Done.” That layout is not confirmed or implemented. Harry's recorded board currently has only “Backlog” and “Ready to be worked on.” [Decisions:174](</Users/harry/Documents/git/ants/docs/mvp/handoff.md:174>), [proposed board:206](</Users/harry/Documents/git/ants/docs/mvp/handoff.md:206>)
2. **Build through review.** Approved work runs on customer Actions infrastructure. Humans are notified only after CI passes and an adversarial AI reviewer considers the PR ready for attention. Default output is one PR per work item, even if agents use subtasks; only an `Epic` label permits child work items with separate PRs. A proposed event-driven repair loop saves sessions as Actions artifacts, exits, and resumes in short jobs after check/review events. Exact retry, cancellation, artifact retention, and merge behavior remain unspecified. [Review and grouping:172](</Users/harry/Documents/git/ants/docs/mvp/handoff.md:172>), [repair proposal:293](</Users/harry/Documents/git/ants/docs/mvp/handoff.md:293>)
3. **Safe intake and spend control.** The recorded model is arm, authorize, preflight, budgets, kill. Only linked members with dispatch authority may trigger work; repositories choose eligible acts. Ticket quality and estimated cost precede execution, with spend/job-count caps per job/day/month and stop mechanisms. Earlier auto-run wording must be reconciled with the later mandatory human plan checkpoint. Trigger catalogs and detailed coaching interfaces remain proposals.
